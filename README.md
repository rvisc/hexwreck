<p class="occhiello">A turn-based duel on a hex board, for two: one die, a hundred hit points
each, and the weapons are lying on the ground.</p>

Hexwreck is a short, sharp duel between two vehicles on a board of hexagons. You roll a die to
find out how far you can move, you race for whatever is lying on the ground, and you shoot when
the other one comes into range.

There is nothing to unlock and nothing to memorise: the rules take five minutes, and after that
only your position matters. A laser reaches three cells, a missile six, the machine gun only what
it touches. A mine is visible to you alone. A robot spider you drop walks towards your opponent on
its own, and it does not stop.

Three ways to play: against the virtual opponent, two players sharing one computer, or two
computers on your home network — and over the network each player reads in their own language.

It works with no internet connection, asks for no account, shows no advertising and collects no
data at all.
